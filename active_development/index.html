Philippines · Property matching platform

Shelter, space, and growth — with people you can trust

HomeFinder helps Filipinos find rentals and workspaces from verified property owners and licensed property brokers, while giving owners and brokers a clear way to list, match, and manage demand — from a first bedspace to a warehouse that keeps a business moving.

Our role

We are not only a listing board. HomeFinder is built so seekers can secure safe shelter and suitable space for living or livelihood, and so owners and licensed brokers can present properties with identity checks, map context, and structured next steps — including contract-aware flows as the platform grows.

Whether someone needs a quiet place to rest, a storefront to open, land to expand, or a logistics node to move goods, the goal is the same: match real need with accountable supply across Philippine cities and communities.

Spaces we help people find

Listings and wanted posts cover living spaces and business infrastructure — so seekers can describe what they need, and providers can show what they actually have.

Who uses HomeFinder

One platform, three primary roles. Your account type shapes what you can post, browse, and unlock with tiers and boosts.

Seeker

Looking for a space

Individuals, families, and operators searching for a place to live, work, sell, or store. Seekers browse listings on Market within their pin radius, save properties when allowed, and post wanted requests that describe budget, area, and move-in timing.

  • Map pin + radius by tier / Seeking Boost
  • Wanted posts under an active cap
  • Path toward contracts and reviews as deals complete
Owner

Listing your own property

People who own or control a property and want serious inquiries — not endless spam. Owners list with photos and details, manage active listings under a free or boosted cap, and browse Wanted requests on the market map within pin radius (boosts add capacity only).

  • Government ID verification before the first listing
  • Listing slots via tier defaults + boosts
  • Reviews and ongoing contract slots as the system matures
Broker

Licensed intermediary

PRC-oriented brokers who represent inventory and clients professionally. Brokers combine listing tools with wanted browsing, and submit PRC / broker certificate details for staff review so seekers see a licensed badge path.

  • ID + PRC / certificate verification
  • List properties and browse wanted demand
  • Portfolio, perks, and boosts for reach

Staff accounts (admin / moderators) review identity documents and payment orders manually — verification is deliberate, not an automatic API rubber-stamp.

From sign-up to a place (and safer deals)

A practical path through the product. Exact screens vary by role; contract tools continue to expand.

1

Create an account

Register with email (or supported social login), then confirm your email via the link that opens our verification page. Log in on login.html.

2

Choose how you show up

Your profile role (seeker, owner, or broker) controls tabs: listing forms, contracts, messages, and perks. Pin, radius, and Wanted discovery live on Market — not a profile map tab.

3

Verify identity (trust layer)

Owners and brokers submit government ID (and brokers, PRC / certificate photos) for staff review. Approval unlocks listing confidence and verified badges. Clear photos without glare speed up review.

4

Discover or publish

Seekers explore the Market and map radius. Owners/brokers publish listings with location pins and photos. Seekers/brokers can also post wanted requests so supply can find demand.

5

Match, message, and document the deal

Contact paths and messaging continue to grow. For real-world safety: meet in public when possible, never pay large sums off-platform without a written agreement, and use HomeFinder’s contract-oriented features as they become available so both sides share a clear record. Completing legitimate contracts is also how tier points progress over time.

6

Grow with perks (optional)

Boost packages and extra listing slots are requested in-app and activated by staff after payment verification (manual BPI / reference flow today). Higher packs expand map radius, wanted caps, listing capacity, and related perks — without forcing every user to pay to start.

Core functions explained

These systems work together so reach and trust scale without turning the app into a paywall-only board.

Market pin & radius

Seekers and brokers set a search center on the map. Listings are emphasized inside a radius that depends on tier and Seeking Boost. Moving the pin is limited by a cooldown so location cannot be spammed; higher boosts can shorten that wait.

Pin relocation cooldown

After you confirm a new map center, a cooldown timer starts (stored on your account). Until it ends, “Move my search spot” stays locked. Status text on the map explains when you can move again.

Boosts & extra slots

Listing Boost and Seeking Boost packages are ordered in-app with a payment reference. Staff verify payment, then activate optional capacity (more listings, wider radius, wanted slots). Market access stays free. Nothing auto-charges a card today — activation is manual by design for this phase.

Wanted on Market

Seekers (and brokers) publish what they need. Owners and brokers browse demand. Active wanted posts are capped by base rules plus Seeking Boost so the feed stays usable.

Verification & badges

Government ID and broker PRC uploads go to staff queues. Approve / reject can include a reason shown in notifications. Verified status is a trust signal — not a guarantee of every offline outcome.

Contracts & tiers

Tier progress is tied to meaningful completed activity (contracts and related rules in the system design), not vanity clicks. Always keep your own copies of receipts and agreements; use in-app contract tools when available so both parties share a timeline.

Safety mindset

HomeFinder adds verification, structured listings, and staff review — but real-world caution still matters.

Do

Prefer verified counterparts when possible. Inspect properties in person when you can. Use written terms for deposits and rent. Keep payment proofs. Report suspicious listings to staff when reporting tools are available.

Don’t

Don’t wire large amounts to strangers solely over chat. Don’t ignore mismatched IDs or pressure tactics. Don’t treat a badge as insurance — it means documents were reviewed, not that every future act is risk-free.
