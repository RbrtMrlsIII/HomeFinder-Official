Privacy Policy

Pre-launch draft: This legal page restores the site's canonical route for testing. The final policy, effective date, retention periods, jurisdiction-specific notices, and contact details must be reviewed and approved before production use.

HomeFinder is designed to help people discover and manage property listings, accounts, messages, assistance requests, and related services. This page describes the privacy topics that the final policy must cover.

Information we may handle

Depending on the features you use, HomeFinder may handle account information, authentication identifiers, property and listing information, messages and requests, map or location information supplied for property discovery, and transaction or entitlement records.

How information is used

Information may be used to provide requested features, authenticate users, operate listings and discovery, support messages and assistance workflows, protect the service, administer subscriptions or boosts, and maintain required records.

Service providers and infrastructure

The application may rely on infrastructure providers for authentication, database storage, hosting/edge delivery, maps, and payment processing. The final policy must identify the applicable providers and describe the relevant data flows before launch.

Security and access

Access to protected data is intended to follow HomeFinder's role and authorization rules. Sensitive operational or payment verification data must remain subject to the application's server-side authorization controls.

Your choices and requests

The final policy will document available account, privacy, correction, deletion, and other applicable rights and the process for requesting them.

Contact

The production contact channel and responsible legal entity are intentionally not invented in this repair patch. They must be supplied before this page is treated as a final policy.
