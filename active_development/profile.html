📖 Messages guide

Purpose. Private person-to-person chat between HomeFinder users. It is not for listing deals — those stay on Contracts.

You can
  • Message someone from user search or their visitor profile (Message button).
  • Filter All, Unread, Archived, and Hidden.
  • Mute notifications, Archive a thread, or Hide it (restore under Hidden).
  • Search your conversation list.
Please don’t
  • Share OTPs, government IDs, or bank details in chat.
  • Treat Messages as a place to close property deals (use Contracts).
  • Confuse Hide chat with Settings → public profile privacy (visitor About only).
Quick map
  • Mute — fewer notifications; chat stays in the list.
  • Archive — stored under Archived.
  • Hide — removed from All; open Hidden to unhide.
