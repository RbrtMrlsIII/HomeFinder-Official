Discover around your pin

Set one or more pins, choose a discovery radius, then narrow the authorized cards with search and filters. Guests can explore the map but do not receive live cards.

  1. 1
    Drop a pin

    Tap Drop pin, place it on the map, then confirm.

  2. 2
    Choose the radius

    The selected pin's radius controls which cards can be discovered.

  3. 3
    Search and filter

    Use type, price/budget and type-aware amenities. Filters read the canonical listing fields.

  4. 4
    Move between pins

    Drop Pin manages separate slots and cooldowns. Center Pin flies to the current pin or cycles to the next.
