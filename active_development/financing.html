What this page is

HomeFinder helps you discover private listings and, separately, learn about public housing finance programs in the Philippines (Pag-IBIG, Expanded 4PH, and related agencies).

We do not approve loans, collect eligibility documents for government programs, or replace Pag-IBIG / DHSUD.

Typical path

  1. Confirm active Pag-IBIG membership and contribution rules on official channels.
  2. Read Expanded 4PH / DHSUD program pages if you are exploring socialized or subsidized options.
  3. Use official portals or branches to check eligibility and apply.
  4. Use HomeFinder Market for private rentals/sales and Contracts for deals between users.
