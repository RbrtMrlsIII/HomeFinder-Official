Every page exit must eventually present:

```text
EXITING: [current page / presentation]

[Cancel] [Continue]
```

The application owns logical source/destination identity and confirmation state. Only after confirmation may door/travel animation begin. The spatial authority owns the actual route, doors, camera transforms, and duration. This applies to same-house and house-transfer navigation.
