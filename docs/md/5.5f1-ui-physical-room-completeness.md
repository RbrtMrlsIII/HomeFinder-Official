# 5.5F.1 — UI → Physical-Room Completeness Census

## Purpose

Determine whether every significant application destination has a logical presentation identity that can be reconciled with physical presentation capacity before attempting an SH3D merge.

**This gate does not require one physical object/room per page, tab, or DOM count.** Logical destinations and physical presentation zones are a many-to-many relationship. A physical object/zone may support multiple UI states through cooperating CSS/JS roots, themes, and animations; a dense logical destination may require multiple presentation zones.

**This gate does not claim that the physical rooms already exist in the canonical model.** It distinguishes logical allocation from physical proof.

## Spatial density finding

`profile.html` is substantially denser than a simple single-purpose page (263 DOM IDs in the current census). Its logical allocation to `PROFILE SUITE` is therefore appropriate, but the eventual physical design may require multiple presentation zones within the suite rather than one camera/room per HTML page.

Likewise, `market.html` and `broker-hq.html` have significant UI density and should not be compressed into arbitrary small rooms.

## What this gate proves

It proves that we have a sufficiently explicit **logical UI destination inventory** to begin comparing against the physical SH3D inputs.

It does **not** prove:

- that every destination has a physical room;
- that every room is suitable;
- that doors connect them;
- that routes are collision-safe;
- that cameras are final;
- or that the models can yet be merged.
