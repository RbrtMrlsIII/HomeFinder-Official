# 5.5G.6I — Architectural Blocker Resolution

## Blocker A — inter-house movement

**Resolved as controlled non-physical transport.**

The canonical SH3D contains three spatially separated house clusters and no inter-house portal evidence. The project therefore does not invent physical doors or modify the SH3D merely to satisfy a navigation requirement. Inter-house movement is represented by an explicit logical transport contract.

House 1 remains the sole inter-house hub. House 2↔House 3 direct travel remains forbidden. Role and identity remain unchanged by travel. Destination access is rechecked through the existing route-access contract.

## Blocker B — protected-freeze drift

**Resolved by explicit freeze reconciliation.**

The previous 5.5G.6C freeze predates the controlled 5.5G.6G canonical promotion. The two changed 3D/topology hashes are preserved as historical evidence, while the active freeze is advanced to `5.5G.6I-1`. Protected rules themselves are unchanged.

## Gate result

5.5G.6I architectural blockers: **RESOLVED**.

Physical runtime routes remain 0 until a future physical-route requirement is introduced with actual portal evidence.
