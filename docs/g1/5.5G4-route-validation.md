## Runtime rule

A route becomes eligible only when:

1. the logical door is identified;
2. the source physical zone is identified;
3. the destination physical zone is uniquely identified;
4. a physical door/portal connects the zones;
5. route continuity can be demonstrated without invented edges.

No camera transition or application authority is changed by this gate.
